# Sample bank statements

Written for FormatReader (fictional accounts and amounts), one per format:
- `mt940-statement.sta`: SWIFT MT940 with German-style ?20 subfields and SWIFT /NAME/ codes in :86:.
- `camt053-statement.xml`: ISO 20022 camt.053.001.08 (Chinese company names and remittance text).
- `checking.ofx`: OFX 1.02 SGML (unclosed leaf tags), as US banks and QuickBooks export.
- `bai2-statement.bai`: BAI2 with opening/closing ledger summaries and an 88 continuation record.
