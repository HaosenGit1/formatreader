# Sample sounds

Made for FormatReader (no third-party content):

- `arpeggio.wav`: a 4-second C–E–G–C arpeggio generated in Python; `arpeggio.m4a` (AAC),
  `.flac` and `.aiff` converted from it with macOS `afconvert`.
- `tone.weba`: a sweeping tone recorded as WebM/Opus by `scripts/make-media-samples.mjs` (Chrome).
