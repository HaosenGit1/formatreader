# Sample e-invoices

Written for FormatReader (fictional companies and numbers):
- `cn-einvoice.xml`: modelled on the structure of China's 数电票 / 全电发票 XML (EInvoice). Field names
  vary between versions and platforms; the reader looks up several known names for each value.
- `ubl-invoice.xml`: UBL 2.1 invoice in the Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 / EN 16931 style.
- `cii-facturx.xml`: UN/CEFACT Cross Industry Invoice, as used by ZUGFeRD / Factur-X / XRechnung CII.
