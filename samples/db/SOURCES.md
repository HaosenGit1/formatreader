- `wind-farm.sqlite`: generated by `scripts/make-db-samples.py`.

  `complexDataTestV2010.accdb`, `linkerTestV2007.accdb` and `unsupportedFieldsTestV2007.accdb`
  from the [Jackcess](https://github.com/jahlborn/jackcess) test data (Apache License 2.0).
