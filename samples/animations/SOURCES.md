# Sample animations

Made by `scripts/make-anim-samples.py` (written by hand, no tools):
- `turbine.json`, `turbine.lottie`, `turbine.tgs`: one Lottie animation (three turbine blades turning,
  2 s at 30 fps) as plain JSON, as a dotLottie package and as a Telegram sticker (gzip).
- `sky.swf`: a minimal Flash file (blue background, a white square).
- `counter.gif`, `counter.png` (APNG), `counter.webp`: 12 numbered frames, 100 ms each (Pillow).
