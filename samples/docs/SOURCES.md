# Document samples

- `noori.hwp`: sample from [hwp.js](https://github.com/hahnlee/hwp.js) (Apache-2.0),
  a public press release of the Korean Ministry of Science and ICT.
- `wind-report.*`: made for FormatReader (python-docx; `.rtf`/`.odt` via macOS
  `textutil`; `.odp`, `.xps`, `.ofd`, `.hwpx`, `.pages`, `.key` generated).
